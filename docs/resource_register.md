# Data Classification

## A. Reference Data
Government statistics, maps, geographic boundaries and environmental datasets.

## B. Operational Data
Data generated by Assam TeaTrace:
- Gardens
- Green leaf lots
- Factory batches
- Warehouse lots
- Auction lots
- Blend batches
- Packages
- Trace events

## C. Evidence Data
Documents and evidence supporting traceability:
- Lab reports
- Quality reports
- Compliance records
- Certificates
- Inspection records

## D. Public Data
Information that can safely be displayed through a consumer QR code.

## E. Private Data
Commercial and sensitive information that requires authorization.


# Important Principle

Official government resources will be used as authoritative reference or contextual data.

Assam TeaTrace will NOT claim government integration, endorsement,
or access to restricted datasets unless formal authorization or an
official API/data-access mechanism is available.
